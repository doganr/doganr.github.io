Summary: An examination of IT law, privacy, intellectual property, and cyber ethics concepts from the perspective of artificial intelligence engineering.

## Course Overview
The primary objective of this course is to equip students with the analytical skills needed to navigate and resolve moral dilemmas they will encounter while developing AI and information technologies. The curriculum bridges the gap between technical development and societal impact.

## Key Topics
* Introduction to IT Ethics & Moral Theories
* Professional Responsibility in Computing
* Data Privacy, Surveillance, and Anonymity
* Intellectual Property Rights in the Digital Age
* Algorithmic Bias and AI Ethics
